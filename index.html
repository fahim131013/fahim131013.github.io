01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity in research.
Purpose in engineering.

I work at the intersection of electromagnetic design, intelligent prediction, and connected systems.

I’m Tanvir Ahmed Fahim, completing my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Bangladesh Army International University of Science and Technology (BAIUST). My research interests include graphene-based THz antennas, MIMO systems, RF and microwave engineering, and machine learning for antenna performance prediction.

Alongside research, I develop embedded and IoT projects. My experience in wireless communication with the Bangladesh Army and industrial training at Nagorik TV adds a practical perspective to the systems I study and build.

LOOKING AHEAD · PhD ASPIRANT

I aspire to pursue a PhD in electrical and electronic engineering, with a focus on antennas, advanced wireless systems, and machine learning. I’m interested in doctoral research opportunities that build on my work in THz and MIMO antenna design.
